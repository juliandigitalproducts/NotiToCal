# Contributing to NotiToCal

Thank you for your interest in NotiToCal.

External contributions (code, translations, documentation, graphics) are only accepted with a signed Contributor
License Agreement (CLA) that grants Julian Digital Products the rights needed to use, relicense and distribute the
contribution, including under other licenses (dual licensing). A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) or a
`Signed-off-by` line is not sufficient for this.

Please get in touch before you start, using the contact address given in the [README](README.md#kontakt--contact).
Bug reports and suggestions are welcome without a CLA.

---

**Deutsch:** Externe Beiträge werden nur mit einem Contributor License Agreement (CLA) angenommen, das Julian Digital
Products die nötigen Rechte einräumt (einschließlich Dual Licensing). Ein DCO reicht dafür nicht. Kontakt über die im
README genannte Adresse.
